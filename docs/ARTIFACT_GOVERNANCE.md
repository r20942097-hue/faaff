# Artifact governance v4

`project_registry.json` is the portfolio source. `PROJECT_INDEX.md` is generated from it; compare exact generated bytes in CI. Product-verified and integration-verified remain distinct. Imported v3 status is historical context, not a new product acceptance result.

Release Manifest v2 identifies the product, version, risk class, source archive and artifact. Evidence records bind product_id, version, source_sha256, artifact_sha256 and a specific product-scope gate. Referenced files must exist and match SHA-256. String booleans, integration-scope records, mismatched versions/products and missing evidence fail closed.

Candidate: source/artifact integrity plus tests, clean extraction, archive integrity, reproducibility, SBOM and provenance records; no candidate-scope blockers. Stable: candidate gates plus externally configured risk-class acceptance/rollback gates, no stable blockers, GO decision, and a non-prerelease SemVer version. This is an eligibility report, not a publication or promotion action.

`policies/promotion-policy.json` controls requirements. Runtime products require real_environment and rollback. Manifest authors cannot switch off these requirements. Review policy edits as changes to acceptance requirements.

Schemas reject unknown fields. Old non-SemVer portfolio labels remain historical references; new release manifests require SemVer. Published identities remain unchanged; v4 does not rename old artifacts.

Local records remain assertions by their producer. Authenticity, full SBOM/provenance schema compliance, real environment acceptance, and signed attestation verification require their respective workflows.
